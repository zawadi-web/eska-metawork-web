Our Story

From a Small Shop to Kenya's Trusted Fabricators

ESKA Metalworks was founded with one vision: deliver industrial-grade metalwork that exceeds client expectations in quality, precision, and durability. Based in Kikuyu, Kenya, we've grown from a small local workshop into one of the region's most respected fabrication companies.

Our founders built this business on craftsmanship, honesty, and hard work. Every beam we weld, every gate we install, every custom piece we fabricate carries those values.

Today, ESKA Metalworks serves residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Kenya — on time and to spec, every time.
