Service 01

Gates & Automation

We design, fabricate, and install premium double-swing, single-swing, sliding, and fully automated steel gates for residential estates, businesses, and homes across Kenya.

With over 500 gates successfully delivered and installed, we ensure heavy-duty hardware, smooth tracks, and weatherproofing for longevity and security.

  • Double-swing & single-swing gates
  • Manual & automated sliding gates
  • Automated gate motors & access control
  • 500+ gates delivered and installed in Kenya

  • Heavy-duty burglar-proof security doors
  • Modern geometric laser-cut steel doors
  • Glass-paneled metal entry doors
